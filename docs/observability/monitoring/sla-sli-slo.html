SLA · SLI · SLO

Internal targets and external commitments for everything the Billpay platform exposes. Numbers are stated at the percentiles we measure to:

P50 P90 P99 P99.9 P99.99

What these terms mean​

SLA — Service Level Agreement

The external commitment to consumers. If breached, it's incident-grade and contractually visible.

SLO — Service Level Objective

The internal target engineering steers to. Tighter than the SLA by design — gives us headroom before customers notice.

SLI — Service Level Indicator

The signal we actually measure — the metric that proves whether we're meeting the SLO.

Quick reference
  • SLA is the promise. SLO is the goal. SLI is the measurement.
  • Latency cells in the sub-pages are SLO targets — internal numbers.
  • The availability pill on each card is the SLA — the external commitment.
  • The "SLI" line under each card describes what we measure to produce these numbers.
  • Numbers are indicative targets — confirm the latest contracted values with the Billpay team before quoting externally.

How to read a card​

Each entry has:

  1. Title — the function or API name (the endpoint sits underneath as a smaller note).
  2. SLA availability pill — the contractual uptime.
  3. A 5-cell latency grid — SLO targets at P50, P90, P99, P99.9, P99.99.
  4. The SLI — the metric + the error budget.

Pick a surface​

📜

One-Data Functions

SLA / SLI / SLO for the 7 versioned, contract-level functions (CreatePayment.v3, UpdatePayment.v1, …). Tighter SLAs because they're the visible surface.

🔌

Billpay Core APIs

SLA / SLI / SLO for the 9 REST endpoints (POST /payments, PUT /payments/:id, …). Slightly tighter latency targets — no function-edge overhead.

Error budget at a glance​

SLA targetAllowable downtime / 30 daysAllowable downtime / year
99.99%~4.3 minutes~52 minutes
99.95%~21.6 minutes~4.4 hours
99.9%~43.2 minutes~8.8 hours
Operationally

The SLO is what engineering steers to. The SLA is what the platform commits to externally. We deliberately leave headroom — if our SLO is being breached but our SLA isn't, that's the cue to fix things before customers notice.
